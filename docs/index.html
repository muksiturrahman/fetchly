Fetchly

A fast download manager with one-click video downloads from Chrome. Many connections per file, resume after anything, YouTube up to 4K. Free.

MacWindowsLinuxAndroidLatest release
🚀 Faster downloadsEvery file comes in parallel parts over up to 32 connections. A connection that finishes early takes over half of the slowest part.
⏯️ Resume after anythingPause, lose Wi-Fi, quit or crash: each part continues exactly where it stopped.
🎬 Videos in one clickPlay a video in Chrome and a Download video bar appears. Pick 4K, 1080p, audio only or MP3. YouTube and ~1,800 sites.
🔒 Private and lightEverything stays on your device. About 20 MB, nothing running in the background, no history kept.
